# Gamakichi autonomy and Kurama swept melee — 2026-11-05

## Gamakichi

The server chooses a nearby visible hostile target without an owner attack. It respects existing group commands: Hold cancels pending strikes/channels and stops autonomous casts; Follow clears the current fight, returns to the owner and gives a two-second recall grace. Command/assist targets have priority when valid and within the owner leash. Acquisition, pursuit and leash distances share the existing companion policy (28/40/48 studs). ForceFields, hostility, line of sight, death and expiry are checked. The toad uses oil on an enemy without an active oil debuff, approaches and uses the adult chopping channel in range, then attacks normally during spell cooldowns. Existing source damage, ranges, cooldowns, lifespan and rank gates are unchanged.

Autonomous requests use the spell state's internal serial. Manual request replay protection has its own counter, so AI cannot make a later genuine player command look stale. Existing R → Z / X manual spells remain available, subject to their shared cooldowns.

Spawn searches nearby collision-free grounded locations instead of clamping the spawn into an obstacle. Navigation respects collidable terrain and ignores noncolliding visual foliage. Explicit gravity runs before Hold/control checks, so anchored toads settle after airborne summons. Humanoid HipHeight is zero and the cosmetic feet align to the navigation clearance plane. No blanket wall collision disabling or teleport-to-target was added.

The old static Appendance mesh has been removed. Gamakichi now uses locally authored articulated geometry: orange skin, purple eye and mouth markings, gold slit eyes, a cream belly, a dark open vest and separate forelegs/hindlegs, head and jaw. Body, limbs and adult sword have distinct breathing, hop, lunge, oil-cast and chopping motions. This is a stylized game adaptation, not an imported anime-production model. It has no external mesh/texture permissions dependency. The alternate marketplace Gamabunta sculpts were reviewed and rejected; Roblox mesh generation returned a moderation failure and was not retried.

Lane curb fix: support is sampled with a shallow plate covering the body footprint instead of a center ray. A bounded 0.65-stud rise/move/lower sweep lets toes cross low road lips; every leg of that route and the final body remain collision checked. Falling also uses the footprint so the center does not immediately drop back down while the toes remain on the road. Pathfinding, walls, ceilings and peer avoidance remain enabled.

## Kurama

Ordinary Naruto M1 remains single-target. Full Kurama evaluates an active sweep from phase .28 to .64 using the exact shared Avatar.Motion transforms and mesh dimensions of the attacking hand/lower arm. Left/right side follows attack serial. The server samples the swept boxes between heartbeats, collects all eligible contacted hurtboxes and keeps a per-attack victim set; there is no arbitrary victim count cap and no repeat damage to one victim during the same swing. Weapons/cosmetics do not extend victim hurtboxes. A teleport cannot leave a damaging trail across the map.

World walls, allies, ForceFields, life/round/profile changes and control cancellation are respected. Damage uses the existing ordinary M1 formula plus the form stat layer exactly once. The independent source shockwave proc retains its own rules. The animation has an outward raised windup, a low cross-body sweep and a distinct recovery, with torso counter-rotation and weight shift. Claw trails use the same active phase interval. Confirmed paw damage publishes an impact timestamp/direction for a 0.44-second torso/head/arm recoil. This is cosmetic, without added stun, forced movement or damage. Client hit-message deduplication now tracks each victim per attack: later swept-contact batches no longer lose their presentation effects. This is an explicit requested gameplay adaptation beyond the source map's single-target attack.
